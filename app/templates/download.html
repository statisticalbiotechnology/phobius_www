Phobius

For a standalone copy of Phobius, see software.sbc.su.se/phobius.html. Phobius is freely available for local installation for academic use.

HomologHMM

HomologHMM, the feature prediction method behind PolyPhobius, is distributed under the GPL, packaged with a slightly modified copy of BioJava, redistributed under the LGPL.

For a standalone copy of PolyPhobius you also need the model file from the Phobius package.

Training data

Phobius was trained on four types of protein, each divided into ten cross-validation sets:

  • Transmembrane proteins with signal peptides: m[0-9].faa
  • Transmembrane proteins without signal peptides: t[0-9].faa
  • Non-transmembrane proteins with signal peptides: s[0-9].faa
  • Non-transmembrane proteins without signal peptides: g[0-9].faa

The sets are in labelled FASTA format.

This web service
